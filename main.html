NEW OFFICIAL PAGE DEBUTS!

Welcome to the new interface.

Posted: August 8, 2026   |   New Changes and Compatibility Updates

Today marks the launch of the new official page for Chres' Castle. This update brings a fresh look and improved navigation, making it easier for visitors to explore our content. We have also ensured that the site is compatible with older browsers and devices, providing a seamless experience for all users.

We are experimenting with compatibility and accessibility features to ensure that our content is available to a wider audience. We welcome feedback and suggestions for further improvements. These experiments are focuced on restrictions within the HTML 4.01 framework, and we are committed to maintaining the integrity of the new design while enhancing usability.

We are also integrating restrictions within Internet Explorer 8 and the Netscape 4.0 largely provided by the PSP NetFront browser for experimental purposes. This will allow us to test how our content performs on older platforms and ensure that our site remains accessible.

This blog will hold a collection of posts, articles, and updates within my personal interests, including independent findings, herbology, and book texts.
