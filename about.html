About Me

Welcome to my little corner of the web. Chres' Castle is a personal blog where I share my research, independent findings, music, and whatever random things happen to be on my mind. Think of it as a digital scrapbook — part journal, part archive, part experiment.

I run the @cdkk.mp3 channel alongside this site, where I post indie music and sound experiments. Here on the blog, I write about the things I'm reading, thinking about, or digging into — from book quotes and wisdom I want to remember, to independent research and herbology notes, to whatever else catches my curiosity.

Why the Old-School Look?
A Note on the Site

You may notice this site is built in plain HTML 4.01, with no JavaScript and a design meant to work on older browsers and devices — including things like Internet Explorer, Netscape, and even the PSP NetFront browser. This is intentional. Part of what I enjoy is testing compatibility and accessibility across older platforms, and keeping the site lightweight enough to run anywhere. Call it a small act of digital preservation, or just a fun experiment in restriction.
